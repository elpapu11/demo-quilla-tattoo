Aftercare

For travelers

Practical guidance for those getting tattooed while traveling.

01

Before your appointment

  • Get good rest the night before
  • Eat a solid meal before coming
  • Stay well hydrated
  • Avoid alcohol 24 hours prior
  • Wear comfortable clothing
02

Aftercare basics

  • Wash with unscented soap and water
  • Apply a thin layer of recommended cream
  • Don't scratch or rub the area
  • Avoid direct sun and pools
  • Let the skin breathe
03

Traveling after your tattoo

  • Protect from sun during hikes
  • Bring moisturizer with you
  • Avoid hot springs and thermal baths
  • Wear loose, non-rubbing clothing
  • Ask your artist about any concerns
✦

Important

Ask your artist for personalized aftercare instructions. Every tattoo and every skin is different.
